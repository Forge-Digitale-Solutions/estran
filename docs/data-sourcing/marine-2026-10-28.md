# Open-Meteo Marine — what the grid actually returns (2026-10-28)

Two live calls to `marine-api.open-meteo.com/v1/marine` with
`hourly=wave_height,wave_period,wave_direction,sea_surface_temperature`,
`timezone=UTC`, `forecast_days=2`. Free, keyless, no account.

## Pointe de Grave (45.570, −1.062) — open Atlantic

| Field | First three hours |
|---|---|
| `wave_height` | 1.16, 1.16, 1.14 m |
| `wave_period` | 11.6, 11.65, 11.7 s |
| `wave_direction` | 278, 279, 279° |
| `sea_surface_temperature` | 15.7, 15.7, 15.6 °C |

48 hourly samples, **no nulls**. Coherent with a west-facing ocean shore: a
11 s swell from the west-north-west, 15.7 °C in late October. Usable as is.

## Arcachon-Eyrac (44.661, −1.169) — inside the Bassin

| Field | First three hours |
|---|---|
| `wave_height` | 2.50, 2.50, 2.50 m |
| `wave_period` | 11.25, 11.25, 11.2 s |
| `wave_direction` | 285, 286, 286° |
| `sea_surface_temperature` | 16.4, 16.4, 16.4 °C |

48 hourly samples, no nulls — and **the wave values are the open-ocean sea, not
the Bassin**. A 2.5 m / 11 s swell cannot cross the passes and stand at the Eyrac
jetty; the model's grid (≈ 0.08° at best, more often 0.25°) does not resolve a
20 km lagoon.

**Consequence.** The sea-surface temperature (16.4 °C) is at least plausible for
the Bassin in October, but the wave fields are not usable there: they would block
surf for the wrong reason and paint a sea that does not exist at the spot. The
SST inside the lagoon therefore also remains **unverified by any independent
source**; it is an interpolated ocean value until a lagoon observation says
otherwise.

## What the app does with it

`MarinePolicy` (`Estran/Data/MarinePolicy.swift`):

- `.offshoreModel` — Pointe de Grave: swell and SST from the model;
- `.unavailable` — Arcachon-Eyrac: no wave data, so the provider throws
  `marineUnavailable` and the screen says « Données indisponibles ». It never
  shows the offshore sea as if it were the lagoon.

**Open decision.** Either the domain learns to carry a missing sea (optional wave
fields in `Swell`, every rule failing closed per activity), or the lagoon drops
the wave-dependent activities from its catalog. Until then the Bassin cannot
answer, and the policy above is the honest state.

## Reproduce

```sh
curl -sS "https://marine-api.open-meteo.com/v1/marine?latitude=45.570&longitude=-1.062\
&hourly=wave_height,wave_period,wave_direction,sea_surface_temperature&timezone=UTC&forecast_days=2"
curl -sS "https://marine-api.open-meteo.com/v1/marine?latitude=44.661&longitude=-1.169\
&hourly=wave_height,wave_period,wave_direction,sea_surface_temperature&timezone=UTC&forecast_days=2"
```
