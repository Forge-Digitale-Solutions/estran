## Notes

- **Z20**: each request is a full 31-day span with an exclusive `dtEnd`; a gap > 1.5 h aborts the run unless the station allowlists it with dates and a source reason.
- **Z21**: `u_h` is one-sided (`max(pred − obs) + 0.05 m`), not `max |Δh|`. `maxAbsoluteErrorMeters` is information only and is never subtracted from a height.
- **Z22**: D2 is a consistency rule — median ≤ 12 min, p95 ≤ 35 min, max |Δt| ≤ `u_t` − 10 min, |bias| ≤ 0.05 m, p95 |Δh| ≤ 0.40 m, max under-prediction ≤ `u_h` − 0.05 m. RMSE is a series property (Z30). The generator aborts if a declared envelope does not cover the measurement.
- **Z23**: the national coefficient uses `range_Brest / 6.10 × 100`, clamped 20…120. The Brest model is **not validated** against a published Annuaire sample (`coefficient.validated = false`), so baignade stays `.dataUnavailable`.
- **Z26**: the 2026-H1 stress test is an anomalous-pressure year, not a horizon measurement. Its bias is weather, not model drift.
- **Z27**: `u_h` is sized on the leave-one-year-out maximum, not on the neutral 2025 year; the `basis` names the worst year.
- **Z28**: `validUntil` is fit end + 3 years for all stations. The horizon bounds astronomical decay (nodal drift), not annual weather dispersion, which `u_h` carries. Regenerate the dataset once a year (BUILD.md).
- **Z29**: `u_t` is sized on the leave-one-year-out max |Δt| + 10 min; the `basis` names the worst year. Extrema are detected on a 1 h smoothed series and plateau-centred on the raw 10-minute series. A 4 cm plateau is still reported at its highest sample: the 2023-08-24 high at station 15 is such a plateau, and `u_t = 75` is kept because the 2023 low-water max is 63.21 min.
- **Z30**: RMSE is a series property — LOYO median ≤ 0.20 m and no year above 0.22 m. The held-out year is one draw, not a regression test.
- Extrema are compared against the validated 10-minute series (source 3), matched within 90 min of the observed extremum.
